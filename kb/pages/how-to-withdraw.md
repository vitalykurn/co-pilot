## Got a question on Depositing and Withdrawing funds?

#### What is the minimum deposit amount?

The minimum deposit starts from 8 USD, depending on the account type.

#### How can I deposit funds into my account?

You may log in to your member area and select the preferred payment option and deposit size.

#### What currencies can I use to deposit funds?

You may deposit using any preferred currency into our USD account, which will follow that day’s exchange rates.

#### How long does it take for deposited funds to be credited to my balance?

Deposits are instant! You will be able to view your updated account balance within 60 minutes.

#### When will my funds be available for trading?

You can begin trading immediately once your balance has been updated.

#### Where can I view my deposit and withdrawal history?

You may log in to your member area to view all transactions related to deposits, withdrawals and transfers.

#### What is my withdrawal request status ‘pending’?

This means your request in in queue and in the midst of getting processed. Once approved, you will be notified.

#### Why was my withdrawal request rejected?

It could be that your account has not been verified, a possible error to data entered or insifficient margin to process withdrawals. We will notify you via email on the rejection reason to help fix this issue.

#### I have not received my funds after my withdrawal request. What should I do?

Should you not receive your funds within 24 hours, please reach out to our friendly support team for assistance.
